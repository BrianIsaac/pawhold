# pawhold

The entry for the PayPal AI Hackathon (deadline 12 November 2026, 12:00 PST). Picked by the owner on 12 October 2026 from the ideas
drafted in the `paypal-ai-hack` repository (Idea 4, Go-Ahead in
`docs/research/ideas-2026-10-11.md`; the research in
`docs/research/hackathon-2026-10-11.md` there).

What it is: hold-then-capture estimates for independent vet practices: the practice sends an estimate, the owner authorises a PayPal hold, the practice captures on completion and re-authorises or voids when the plan changes, and an AI drafts the estimate from the clinical notes and explains every change in the owner's words. Aimed at Most Impactful, Bryntum as the sponsor prize.

Status: named and registered, 12 October 2026. Nothing is built; the plan
comes first (`docs/plans/`), then the spike the research names, then the
units.
